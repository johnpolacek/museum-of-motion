Museum of Motion

A fictional museum whose exhibits are the 63 effect recipes in the Animaxxing skills, grouped by what drives the motion: time, scroll, pointer, or drag.

Each direction shows the same content: the landing view, the full collection, one exhibit page (01 Line mask), and About. These are static layouts. Play buttons, scrub bars, and hover states are drawn but don’t work yet.

  • A

    Index

    Swiss type directory on white. The landing view lists every exhibit by number. The exhibit page draws a paused frame as a diagram.

    Geist · Geist Mono
  • B

    Notation

    Dark specimen sheet. Every exhibit is drawn as a small motion plot. The exhibit page pairs the real timing curve with a frame and a film strip.

    IBM Plex Sans · IBM Plex Mono
  • C

    Poster

    Cobalt field, large type. Each word of the headline is caught mid-effect and numbered to its exhibit.

    Archivo
  • D

    Stack

    Yellow field, condensed capitals. Three ruled headline lines, one exhibit per line.

    Big Shoulders Display · Inter Tight
  • E

    Rooms

    A color for each group. The landing plays one exhibit at a time on the headline, with a pager.

    Bricolage Grotesque
  • F

    Annotated

    Black field, wide type. Callouts with leader lines name the exhibit on each word.

    Unbounded · JetBrains Mono
